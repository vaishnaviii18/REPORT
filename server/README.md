# The Report

A weekly journal for friend groups. Every week each person fills in their **R-E-P-O-R-T**:

- **R**adio: what you're listening to or reading
- **E**ating
- **P**laying
- **O**bsessed with
- **R**ecommend
- **T**reating myself

Each letter can have text, a photo, a voice note and a video (up to 1 minute).
Reports are sent by Saturday and revealed to the group on Sunday. There's also a
**Daily snap**: one photo a day from everyone, on a calendar.

## Folders

- `server/`: the backend (Node, Express, MongoDB). See `server/README.md`.
- `client/`: the app screens (React). See `client/README.md`.
